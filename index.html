What is SlimCrystal?

SlimCrystal is a reusable glass water bottle with a sealed central chamber holding a blend of natural crystals, including stones like amethyst, quartz and green aventurine. The idea comes from crystal-healing traditions: the maker says water that sits alongside these stones supports your weight-loss goals, digestion and energy.

The routine the brand suggests is simple. Drink 2–3 litres of water from the bottle every day, and keep it up for at least 60 days.

Straight talk: No solid scientific evidence shows that crystals change water or cause weight loss directly. What is well supported is that drinking enough water helps with fullness, energy and cutting sugary drinks. SlimCrystal's real strength is that it turns that habit into a daily ritual you'll want to keep.
